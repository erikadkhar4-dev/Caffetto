About the cafe

A cozy stop, a well-travelled menu

Tucked into Aashiyana Infinity right on the Old Mumbai–Pune highway, Caffetto is the kind of stop drivers plan their route around. Inside, it's all warm wood, hanging greens and easy conversation — the sort of room that invites you to slow down for a bit, whether you're refuelling mid-drive or settling in with friends.

The kitchen doesn't stick to one cuisine. On the same table you'll find hand-tossed pizza, steamed bao, a house ramen bowl, crisp bravas and hand-poured coffee — comfort food from a few different corners of the world, plated with care and priced to keep you coming back.
